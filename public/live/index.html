Nils Mango Live

Live Electronic Music, Created in the Moment.

This is Nils Mango Live, a podcast featuring fully live electronic music performances by Nils Mango and guests.

Nothing is prerecorded. Every sound is created live, in real time, mostly using 7III Tap to control Ableton Live.

Find this podcast everywhere you are listening to podcast. The normal sessions are broadcast live on YouTube whenever the internet allows it. All live sessions are also getting uploaded to the Nils Mango Live YouTube playlist.

There are two recurring formats:

Tracks on a Plane Live performances recorded airplanes. Portable setup (7III Tap on iPhone), limited conditions, full commitment to the moment.

Normal Sessions Live sessions recorded in Switzerland and other places around the world.

Expect techno, electronica, ambient, and occasional experimental journeys.
