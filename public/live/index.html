Nils Mango Live

Live electronic music, created in the moment.

Nils Mango Live is a podcast featuring fully improvised live electronic music performances.

I come completely unprepared. Nothing is prerecorded. Everything is created live in real time using 7III Tap to control Ableton Live.

The guiding principle of Nils Mango Live: I didn’t prepare for this, let’s make some music.

Find this podcast everywhere you are listening to podcast by searching for "Nils Mango Live", or finding your player in the list below:

  • Listen on Apple Podcasts
  • Listen on Overcast
  • Listen on Castbox

Every session is also getting uploaded to YouTube and the Nils Mango Live YouTube playlist.

There are two recurring formats:

  • Tracks on a Plane - Live performances recorded airplanes. Portable setup (7III Tap on iPhone), limited conditions, full commitment to the moment.
  • Normal Sessions - Live sessions recorded in Switzerland and other places around the world.

Expect techno, electronica, ambient, and occasional experimental journeys.
