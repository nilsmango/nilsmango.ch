I just released a simple but super useful app I built for my own weight training: [Plates Calc](https://project7iii.com/plates-calc/). Plates Calc is available for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.  
It’s free (for now), works offline, and keeps your data completely private. 

With Plates Calc, you can:
- Create dumbbell and barbell setups, or kettlebells and machines
- Add your weights in kg or pounds
- Instantly see the total weight in your chosen unit and how many pairs of plates are loaded

Whether you’re lifting at home or in the gym, this app helps you stay focused on your workout instead of doing math.

[Download](https://apps.apple.com/app/7iii-plates-calc/id6742782121) it on the App Store, try it out and let me know what you think!
