The first **Tracks on a Plane** installment of the Nils Mango Live podcast. Entirely improvised and played live, nothing prerecorded, on a plane ride from Basel to Tenerife (EJU1025). Some turbulence, and a pretty wild ride.  
Used in this session: iPhone with [7III Tap](https://project7iii.com/tap), [Ableton Live](https://ableton.com/live). All synths stock Ableton Live instruments.
